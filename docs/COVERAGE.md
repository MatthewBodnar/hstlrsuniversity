## Editing rules

- Public reading copy contains no original creator, course or exact company identities. Original transcripts and private working notes are excluded from the repository.
- Narration speaks directly to the reader. Reporting and source attribution stay outside the reading page.
- Profanity is masked by replacing a vowel with a star. The same masking is applied to titles, search text and diagram labels.
- Historical anecdotes remain first-person stories. Hypothetical projects remain conditional, rather than being invented as completed achievements. Unclear currency remains unspecified.
- Short direct statements preserve the distinction between available cash and obligations, actual value and fabricated promises, genuine urgency and invented stock. No jurisdiction-specific legal or financial rules are inferred from anecdotes.
- Arithmetic is made explicit: 3 × 40,000 = 120,000; 10% = 12,000 commission; 200,000 / 5,000 = 40 small attempts; a 20% → 30% margin change is ten percentage points; 5,000 → 4,500 is a 500 reduction.
- Two interactive illustrations retain a fixed 100 delivery cost for the website example and a 10,000 starting reserve for runway. They are labelled examples, rather than additional historical facts.
- There are no quizzes, assessments, scores, or test sections in the reading experience.
